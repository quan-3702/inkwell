🖋️ How to use Inkwell

Inkwell is a private diary. Everything you write stays on this PC – there is no account and nothing is uploaded.

Write

Click ✍️ Write today (or press Ctrl+N). Pick how you feel, add a title if you like, and start writing. Inkwell saves as you type. Change the date or time at the top to write about another day.

Formatting: select text and click B or I (or Ctrl+B / Ctrl+I). H makes a heading, • List a list and ❝ a quote. You can also type **bold**, *italic*, ## Heading, - item and > quote.

Stuck? Click 💡 Writing ideas for a prompt, or answer Today's prompt above your timeline.

Tags, moods and favourites

Type a #tag anywhere – like #family, #work or #travel. Tags and moods appear on the left; click one to see only those entries. Click ☆ to mark an entry as a favourite.

Photos

Click 📷 Add photo. Photos are resized to save space and kept inside Inkwell. The free version keeps one photo per entry; Pro adds as many as you like. All photos also appear under 🖼 Photos.

Look back

📅 Calendar shows the days you wrote, coloured by mood. 🕰 On this day (Pro) shows what you wrote on the same day in earlier years. 📊 Insights (Pro) shows your streaks, moods, year in pixels and top tags.

PIN lock

☰ More → PIN lock. Choose 4–8 digits and when Inkwell should lock again. Press Ctrl+L or 🔒 to lock right away.

Inkwell cannot recover a forgotten PIN. Make a backup now and then – a backup does not contain the PIN.

Back up, export and print

☰ More → Back up everything saves one file with all journals, entries and photos. Use Restore a backup on this or another PC. Export as text saves a .txt file. With Pro you can print your journal as a book – choose “Save as PDF” in the print window – or export it to Word.

Several journals (Pro)

☰ More → New journal to keep separate diaries – personal, travel, dreams, gratitude. Switch journals at the top.

Paper and fonts

☰ More → Paper & font: cream or white paper, Georgia or Segoe UI and three text sizes. Pro adds sepia and night paper and handwriting and typewriter fonts.

Inkwell Pro

Pro is a one-time purchase through the Microsoft Store – no subscription. You can try every Pro feature free for 7 days. Bought Pro on another PC? Click ★ → Restore purchase.
