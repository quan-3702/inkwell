Inkwell – Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

Inkwell does not collect, send or sell any personal information. Your diary entries, moods, tags and photos are stored only in the app's private storage on your own device. They are never uploaded anywhere – Inkwell has no server, no account and no cloud sync.

Inkwell has no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Photos you add are read from the files you choose and saved inside the app on this device.

The optional PIN lock is never stored as written: Inkwell keeps only a salted SHA-256 fingerprint of it to check the PIN you type. The PIN keeps other people from opening the app on your PC; it is not a replacement for your Windows account password.

Backups and exports (.json, .txt, .doc, PDF) are created only when you ask for them and are saved where you choose. Keep backup files somewhere safe – they contain your entries.

Purchases of Inkwell Pro are handled by the Microsoft Store. Inkwell only asks the Store whether Pro is owned; it never sees your payment details.

Uninstalling Inkwell deletes all of its stored data from this device.
